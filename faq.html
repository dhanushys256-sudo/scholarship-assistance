Eligibility Questions

Who can apply for scholarships on ScholarBridge? +

Any Indian student studying from Class 8 to PhD level can register and apply. We have scholarships for students from all categories — General, OBC, SC, ST — and special scholarships for girls, students with disabilities, and students from economically weaker sections.

Is there an income limit to apply? +

Income limits vary by scholarship. Most government scholarships have income criteria ranging from ₹1 LPA to ₹8 LPA. Some merit-based scholarships have no income limit. Use our Eligibility Checker to find scholarships that match your family income.

Can I apply for multiple scholarships at once? +

Yes! You can apply for multiple scholarships simultaneously. However, some government schemes may not allow you to hold multiple government scholarships at the same time. Private scholarships generally don't have this restriction.

What is the minimum percentage required? +

The minimum percentage varies: Merit-based scholarships typically require 75–85%. Need-based scholarships may only require a passing grade. Some like INSPIRE require top 1% in board exams. Check each scholarship's specific criteria.

📝

Application Process

How do I track my application status? +

Log in to your ScholarBridge account and visit the Dashboard. Under "My Applications," you can see the real-time status of each application: Pending → Under Review → Approved or Rejected. You'll also receive notifications for status updates.

Can I edit my application after submission? +

You cannot edit a submitted application for most scholarships. If you need to make corrections, contact our support team immediately via the Contact page. If the scholarship is still in "Pending" status, we may be able to assist.

What file formats are accepted for document upload? +

We accept PDF, JPG, and PNG files. Each file must be under 5MB. Make sure documents are clearly scanned/photographed and all text is legible. Low-quality documents may lead to application rejection.

How long does the approval process take? +

Processing times vary: Government scholarships typically take 2–3 months. Private scholarships may take 3–6 weeks. You can track your status in the Dashboard. Contact support if there's no update for more than 90 days.

Payment & Renewal

How is the scholarship amount disbursed? +

Most scholarships are disbursed directly to your registered bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Some are paid to your educational institution directly. Always ensure your bank account is linked to your Aadhaar.

Do I need to renew my scholarship every year? +

Yes, most multi-year scholarships require annual renewal. You need to submit proof of enrollment, updated marksheets, and confirm your income details each year. We'll send you renewal reminders 60 days before the deadline.

Is ScholarBridge free to use? +

Absolutely! ScholarBridge is 100% free for students. We charge no registration fees, no application fees, and no commission. Our mission is to ensure every deserving student gets access to financial support for education.

Technical Help

I forgot my password. How do I reset it? +

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your registered email address. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact our support team.

The website is slow or not loading. What should I do? +

Try refreshing the page (Ctrl+R / Cmd+R), clearing your browser cache, or using a different browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended). If problems persist, check our status page or contact support.
